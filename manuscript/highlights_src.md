---
title: "Highlights"
---

- India changed the expiry weekday of index options many times in 2016–2025.
- These changes serve as natural experiments on the effects of options expiry.
- Expiry raises volatility over the whole trading day little, if at all.
- Settlement-window (15:00–15:30) volatility is 53–67% higher on expiry days.
- This effect moves with the expiry weekday and stayed large after SEBI's 2024 rules.
